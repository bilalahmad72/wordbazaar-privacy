Privacy Policy - Word Bazaar

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Developer: Bilal Ahmad ("we", "us")

Contact: bilalahmad72.official@gmail.com

Word Bazaar ("the app") is a free word puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

1. What we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or location.
  • We do not run our own servers. Your game progress is not sent to us.

2. What stays on your device

The app saves your game on your device only: coins, hints, level stars, the daily reward streak and your sound, music and vibration settings. This data is stored in the app's private storage and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded video ads (you choose to watch one to get free coins or a hint). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, app version),
  • ad interaction and diagnostics data.

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the app asks for your consent before ads are requested, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice later and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Learn how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Children

The app is a general-audience word game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through ads, contact us and we will help.

5. Security

Saved game data is protected on the device against tampering. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure.

6. Your choices

  • Decline or change ad consent in the consent form (where shown).
  • Reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Delete all local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available at this page, and the effective date above changes when we do.
